# Groww Starter: app link

**Live app:** https://ayanshs007.github.io/groww-starter/

## How to review

1. On the Landing page, tap **Reviewer? Load a demo**.
2. **Riya** shows dips, Steady mode and the Stop coach. **Meera** shows goals.
3. In Reviewer tools, try the **Mood** control and **Unlock Pro**.

## Offline copy

[`groww-starter.html`](groww-starter.html) in this folder runs offline. Open it in a browser. No server or internet is needed.
