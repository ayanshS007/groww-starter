# User test session script (15 min per person)

## Setup (before they arrive)
- Open https://ayanshs007.github.io/groww-starter/ on YOUR phone in incognito. Reviewer tools → Reset.
- Have a notes sheet and a timer.
- Rules for me: don't help, don't explain, don't defend. If they're stuck for 60 seconds, give one hint and mark the task "hinted".

## Intro (say this)
"I'm testing an app, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please think out loud as you go: say what you're looking for and what confuses you."
Before you start: "On a scale of 1–5, how confident would you feel making your first investment today?" → write down.

## Tasks
T1 (Evals #1–3) Fresh account.
"Imagine you earn ₹28,000 a month and want to start investing. Use the app to get started."
Record: completed? time? wrong turns?
Then ask: "Why do you think the app suggested this?" (pass = mentions 2+ of their own answers)
and: "What's the main risk of this fund?" (pass = roughly correct)

T2 (Eval #4) Reviewer tools → load Riya.
"Your portfolio dropped this week. What would you do?"
Record: did they choose to stop or sell without a real reason?

T3 (Eval #5) Still Riya.
"Rent is due and money is tight this month. Handle your SIP."
Record: skip / pause / lower amount / stop?

T4 (Eval #6) Still Riya.
"You just got paid ₹28,000. What would you do in the app?"
Record: did they find "Got paid? Split it"?

T5 (Eval #10) Still Riya → Dashboard.
"Where is most of your money right now, and is your emergency fund on track?"
Record: both answered correctly?

T6 (Eval #8) Explore → Stocks.
"A friend says you should buy this stock today. What do you do?"
Record: did they check (Tip Check / read detail) before buying?

## Closing questions
- "Did the Stop screen feel like it was trying to trap you? 1–5" (Eval #9)
- "Now, how confident would you feel making your first investment? 1–5" (Eval #11)
- "Does this feel like Groww? 1–5" (Eval #12)
- "What's one thing you'd change?"
